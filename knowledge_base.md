**Q: What is a short sale?**
A: A short sale is when a homeowner sells their property for less than what they owe on the mortgage, with the lender's approval, to avoid foreclosure.

**Q: What is an REO property?**
A: REO (Real Estate Owned) properties are homes that have been foreclosed and are now owned by the bank. They're often priced below market value.

**Q: What is a HUD Home?**
A: A HUD home is a property that was purchased with an FHA loan and then foreclosed. HUD (U.S. Dept. of Housing and Urban Development) takes ownership and sells them, often at competitive prices.

**Q: Can you help investors?**
